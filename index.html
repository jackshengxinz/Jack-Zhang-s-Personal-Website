About Me

I’m an M.S. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Varun Chandrasekaran. I received my B.S. in Computer Engineering with a minor in Statistics from UIUC in 2026.

I’m interested in when language models make confident but unreliable judgments, and how to design evaluations that catch those failures. I’m also exploring how AI agents use tools, check their work and recover when a plan fails, and how embodied agents use feedback from simulation and the physical world.

Research interests: LLM safety, LLM agent evaluation, Multi-agent collaboration, Simulation for embodied AI.
